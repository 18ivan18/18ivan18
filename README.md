### About me

- 🛠️ **5+ years building production software.** Most recently a backend engineer on the blockchain team at **Nexo**: microservices in TypeScript/Node.js, Kafka, and AWS infrastructure managed with Terraform. Before that, four years of full-stack product work at **Astea Solutions**.
- 🧠 **ML background.** MSc in Information Retrieval from Sofia University. My thesis was on automated screening for macular degeneration and diabetic retinopathy with CNNs and camera-specific model training.
- 🎓 **Teaching assistant at FMI, Sofia University, since 2019.** Artificial Intelligence, Data Structures and Programming, Object-Oriented Programming, Introduction to Programming and Discrete Mathematics.
- 🏆 **Organising Bulgaria's first National Olympiad in AI** for students in grades 8–12.
- 🎄 I solve Advent of Code every December, and I'm always up for a hackathon.
